Job Ledger privacy policy

Draft. This policy describes the Job Ledger Android app and takes effect when it launches on Google Play. Last updated October 21, 2026.

Job Ledger (Android) is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Questions or requests: the support email shown on the Play Store listing.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. You don't give your name or email to use the app.
  • Everything you enter is stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere by the app itself.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling of data.
  • Information leaves your phone only when you send it: a backup or CSV file you save or share, or an "Add to calendar" link you tap — which opens Google Calendar carrying the event details you typed.
  • The Android app does not sync to Google Drive and does not send wording requests anywhere; those features exist only in other builds, not on Play. (Drive sync is planned for a later release; this policy will be updated if it ships.)
  • You can delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

What the app stores on your phone

Whatever you choose to track, for example:

  • companies, job titles, and application stages;
  • contacts and interview details (dates, times, locations, notes);
  • follow-up dates and prep checklists;
  • your resume text and posting text you paste in, and the match results computed from them;
  • app settings, including your free/Pro state.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which Android keeps apart from other apps. Your phone's own encryption protects the stored data.

Match results are computed on your phone from the resume and posting text you provide. In testing they agreed with hand-written labels 80% of the time on a 25-label held-out set and never called a real gap a match (misses lean cautious) — a rough guide, not a guarantee, and not a promise about any real application.

When information leaves your phone

Only in these cases, each started by you:

Backups and exports. The JSON backup and CSV export are created on your phone from your data. They go wherever you save or send them (your files, an email, a messaging app). The developer never receives them. Treat the files as private: a backup contains everything you entered, including your resume text.

"Add to calendar" links. On an interview or follow-up reminder you can tap "Add to Google Calendar" (or "Add to calendar"). That opens a Google Calendar page carrying the event details you entered — title, company, date and time, and any notes — so Google receives that text when you tap. Nothing is sent before you tap.

Optional one-time Pro upgrade. Job Ledger is free for up to 20 tracked applications (closed jobs count), including the resume matcher, backup/restore, and calendar export. A single one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited applications. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the developer receives the purchase record through Play under Google's terms, but no payment details and nothing you entered in the app.

What I don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics libraries in the app.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no profiling.
  • No selling or renting of your data, and no sharing of it beyond what you send yourself (above).
  • No automatic crash reporting from the app itself.
  • No account, no sign-in, and no server of mine that stores your data.
  • In the Android app: no Google Drive sync and no wording-suggestion requests. (The phone web app and computer server versions offer optional Drive sync to your own Drive and optional wording suggestions using an API key you provide; those are not in the Android app.)

Google Play and Android

Google Play shows developers aggregated statistics — install counts, device types, countries — and, where you allow it in your Android settings, crash and performance reports (Android vitals). Google collects these under its own privacy policy; they don't include what you track in the app. I use them only to find and fix problems.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete single entries in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling Job Ledger or going to Android Settings > Apps > Job Ledger > Storage > Clear storage (called Clear data on some phones). This can't be undone, so save a backup first if you want to keep anything.
  • Backup and CSV files you saved: delete them where you saved them.

Because there is no account, there is no account to delete.

Children

Job Ledger is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly receive information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The only personal data I hold is what you send me by email at the support address. To make a request, email that address.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and the app will mention important changes in its update notes.
